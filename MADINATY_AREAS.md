# Madinaty area directory

The app's location filter now uses recognizable Madinaty area names instead of numbered placeholders.

## Residential areas

- Apartment groups: `B1`, `B2`, `B3`, `B6`, `B7`, `B8`, `B9 + B13`, `B10`, `B11`, `B12`, `B14`, `B15`
- Privado
- Villa groups: `VG1`, `VG2`, `VG3`, `VG4`, `VG5`
- The Lake Villas

## Commercial and destination areas

Craft Zone, East Hub, Open Air Mall, Arabesque, South Park, Central Park, Banks Area, All Seasons Park, The Strip, Madinaty Central, Medical Center, Madinaty City Hall, Main Bus Station, Madinaty Golf Club, Madinaty Sporting Club, and Four Seasons Madinaty.
